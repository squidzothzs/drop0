01 — the message

what is
mogi.

a face with four teeth that has been here longer than the label on it.

you either see it or you don’t.

the head is drawn new for every drop. that one never comes back.

keep the shirt simple. shrink it to a tag on the hem. it still exists.

paper, spray, ipad. never a stock file.

numbered. trade-locked. miss it and you missed it.

draw it → make twenty → gone. no restock. ever.
