01 — WHAT IS MOGI

What is
MOGI.

MOGI is where everything I build comes together in one place — the art, the clothes, the community, the systems, the business — under one identity.

one luxury streetwear identity, built on all of it at once.

I draw MOGI constantly. Every drop carries a new version of the same face, a different piece of the same ongoing work. This is where the art actually lives.

It’s also where I build community — the same instinct that ran a 900-person crew inside a game growing up, now applied to something real.

It’s where my own fashion sense shows up directly: clean pieces carrying something strange on top. My own read on what luxury streetwear actually means, not someone else’s.

And it’s where the parts most people don’t see get built — scarcity, systems, how a drop actually runs, how a business actually runs. That’s as much a part of MOGI as the artwork is.

not one skill applied to a category — everything I’m good at, at the same time.

02 — WHERE IT GOES

Where it
goes.

The clothes were the proof. What we were really testing was the system behind them — every release numbered, nothing ever restocked, and every owner recorded so a piece can be traded on later. Apparel was just the first thing we ran it on.

Same rules, new objects

Next the system moves to things you don’t wear. First is a collectible MOGI figure, already in development — made for people who want the object itself, not just something to put on.

Then it moves onto this site

The step after that isn’t a product at all. Right now every claim happens by hand: a DM, a waitlist, a reply from us. That whole process is moving here — not a normal shop page, but a claim system we build ourselves. You’ll watch pieces get claimed as it happens, see who owns what, and trade directly with other owners, all on one page in real time.

What never changes

One rule holds through all of it: there is never more. Every release stays numbered and finite. The only things that grow are the kinds of objects the system covers, and how much of it you can see from the outside.

there is never more of it. only more places it shows up.
